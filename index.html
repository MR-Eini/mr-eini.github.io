About

I am an environmental engineer and water resources specialist focused on protecting water security in a changing climate. My research blends process-based modeling (SWAT, SWAT-MODFLOW), machine learning, and satellite observations to deliver actionable drought and flood intelligence.

International collaborations across Italy, Germany, and Poland have shaped my approach to interdisciplinary, stakeholder-ready science. I enjoy translating technical insight into practical guidance for resilient, equitable water management.

Research Focus

Modeling

Advanced Hydrology

Process-based simulations for karst and peatland systems; machine learning for drought and flood prediction using satellite data.

Climate

Climate Risk & Adaptation

Extreme event analysis, future scenario design, and decision support for adaptive risk reduction and crop resilience.

Policy

Basin-scale water accounting and socio-economic framing to guide equitable allocation and long-term sustainability.

Satellite precipitation, soil moisture, evapotranspiration, and peat displacement monitoring for real-time hydrologic intelligence.
